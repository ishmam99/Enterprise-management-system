POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking an Aerospace Engineer specializing in Acoustics and NVH to join 
our MSC Software team. This role focuses on Actran software for aircraft cabin 
noise, engine acoustics, and ICAO Chapter 14 noise certification compliance.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Conduct Actran training focused on aircraft cabin noise and engine acoustics

• Provide technical support for ICAO Chapter 14 noise certification compliance

• Drive aerospace sales through noise footprint and community noise 
  demonstrations

• Develop workflows for interior cabin comfort and passenger noise exposure 
  analysis

• Support customers in aero-acoustic analysis (trailing edge, landing gear 
  noise)

• Assist in vibro-acoustic coupling for structural-borne noise prediction

• Collaborate with aerospace OEMs on engine noise propagation and nacelle 
  design

• Present case studies on helicopter rotor noise and propeller acoustics

  • Background in aircraft interior or propulsion acoustics
