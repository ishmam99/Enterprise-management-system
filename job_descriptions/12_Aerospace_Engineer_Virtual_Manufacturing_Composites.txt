================================================================================
POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking an Aerospace Engineer specializing in Virtual Manufacturing and 
Composites to join our MSC Software team. This role focuses on Marc software 
for aerospace composite manufacturing including autoclave curing, AFP/ATL, and 
out-of-autoclave (OOA) processes.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Conduct Marc training for autoclave curing and composite manufacturing 
  processes

• Provide technical support for aerospace composite layup and resin transfer 
  molding

• Drive aerospace sales through composite airframe manufacturing demonstrations

• Develop workflows for cure cycle optimization and spring-in prediction

• Support customers in reducing composite part scrap and manufacturing time

• Assist in out-of-autoclave (OOA) process simulation and validation

• Collaborate with aerospace manufacturers on automated fiber placement (AFP)

• Present case studies on residual stress in composite structures and tool 
  design

================================================================================

  • Experience with composite airframe manufacturing and tooling
  • Background in aircraft OEM or composite Tier-1 supplier
