POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking an Aerospace Engineer specializing in Fluids and FSI to join 
our MSC Software team. This role focuses on Dytran software for bird strike 
certification, ditching analysis, and foreign object damage (FOD) simulation 
in the aerospace sector.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Conduct Dytran training for bird strike certification and ditching analysis

• Provide technical support for foreign object damage (FOD) and ice impact 
  simulation

• Drive aerospace sales through FAA bird strike and water impact demonstrations

• Develop workflows for fuel sloshing in aircraft tanks and thermal 
  aero-heating

• Support customers in meeting bird strike certification requirements 
  (CS-25, FAR-25)

• Assist in coupled FSI analysis for engine ingestion and blade-out scenarios

• Collaborate with aerospace teams on thermal protection system design

• Present case studies on ditching performance and emergency landing 
  simulations
