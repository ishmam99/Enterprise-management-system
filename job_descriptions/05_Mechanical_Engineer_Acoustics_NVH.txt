================================================================================
POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking a Mechanical Engineer specializing in Acoustics and NVH (Noise, 
Vibration, and Harshness) to join our MSC Software team. This role focuses on 
Actran software for interior/exterior acoustics and vibro-acoustic analysis in 
automotive, HVAC, and appliance industries.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Deliver Actran training for interior/exterior acoustics and vibro-acoustic 
  analysis

• Provide technical support for NVH troubleshooting and noise reduction 
  strategies

• Drive sales in automotive, HVAC, and appliance industries for noise 
  compliance

• Develop workflows for sound quality prediction and acoustic treatment 
  optimization

• Support customers in meeting industrial noise regulations and standards

• Conduct demonstrations on cabin noise, engine noise, and vibration analysis

• Assist in acoustic material modeling and absorption coefficient validation

• Create technical content on NVH best practices and simulation techniques

================================================================================
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
================================================================================

EDUCATION:
  • B.S./M.S. in Mechanical, Acoustical Engineering or Physics

EXPERIENCE:
  • 3+ years in NVH analysis or acoustic simulation
  • Experience with Actran, LMS, or similar acoustic software
  • Knowledge of automotive or industrial NVH requirements
  • Understanding of vibro-acoustics and sound transmission
