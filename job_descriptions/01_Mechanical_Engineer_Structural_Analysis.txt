================================================================================
POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking a talented Mechanical Engineer specializing in Structural 
Analysis to join our MSC Software team. This role combines technical expertise 
in FEA simulation with customer-facing responsibilities including training 
delivery, technical support, and sales support. You will work with 
manufacturing and industrial clients to help them leverage MSC Nastran, Marc, 
and other structural analysis tools to solve complex engineering challenges.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Conduct MSC Nastran/Marc training for structural FEA and nonlinear analysis

• Provide technical support for complex structural simulations and 
  troubleshooting

• Drive new sales by demonstrating FEA solutions to manufacturing and 
  industrial clients

• Develop simulation workflows for static, thermal, and dynamic structural 
  problems

• Assist customers in model validation and results interpretation

• Collaborate with sales team to identify customer needs and propose MSC 
  solutions

• Create technical documentation and application examples for structural 
  analysis

• Support pre-sales demonstrations and proof-of-concept projects

================================================================================
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
================================================================================

EDUCATION:
  • B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (M.S. preferred)

EXPERIENCE:
  • 3+ years hands-on FEA experience with MSC Nastran or Marc
  • Strong knowledge of structural mechanics and material properties
  • Experience in manufacturing, automotive, or heavy machinery industries
  • Proven ability to deliver technical training and presentations

Professional Skills:

  • Willingness to travel for client engagements
