================================================================================
POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking an experienced Aerospace Engineer specializing in Structural 
Analysis to join our MSC Software team. This role focuses on supporting 
aerospace OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers with MSC Nastran-based solutions for 
aircraft certification, aeroelastic analysis, and composite structures. You 
will deliver technical training, provide expert support, and drive sales in 
the aerospace sector.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Deliver MSC Nastran training focused on aeroelastic analysis and aircraft 
  certification

• Provide expert technical support for aerospace structural integrity and 
  flutter analysis

• Drive aerospace sector sales through FAA/EASA certification workflow 
  demonstrations

• Develop simulation best practices for composite airframe and fuselage 
  analysis

• Support customers in meeting aerospace certification requirements 
  (DO-160, etc.)

• Conduct fatigue and damage tolerance analysis for aircraft components

• Collaborate with aerospace OEMs to implement MSC solutions in design 
  processes

• Present technical webinars on aerospace-specific MSC Software applications
