POSITION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are seeking a Mechanical Engineer with expertise in Multibody Dynamics 
(MBD) to join our MSC Software team. This role focuses on Adams software for 
machinery, robotics, and mechanical system simulation. You will deliver 
training, provide technical support, and drive sales in automotive, heavy 
equipment, and industrial machinery sectors.

================================================================================
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

• Deliver Adams MBD training for machinery, robotics, and mechanical system 
  simulation

• Provide technical support for kinematics, dynamics, and control system 
  integration

• Drive sales in automotive, heavy equipment, and industrial machinery sectors

• Develop multibody dynamics workflows for mechanism design and optimization

• Support customers in virtual prototyping and testing of mechanical systems

• Conduct pre-sales demonstrations showcasing Adams motion analysis 
  capabilities

• Assist in co-simulation setup between Adams and FEA/CFD tools

• Create application examples for landing gear, suspension, and linkage 
  mechanisms
